# Age ratings and content compliance

Reviewed: 2026-08-29

Ratings describe the shipped experience, including content players can encounter through gameplay, dialogue, cinematics, UGC, online interaction and monetization. Storefront questionnaires are attestations; answers must be traceable to evidence.

## Content inventory

Track violence, fear/horror, language, sex/nudity, substances, gambling/simulated gambling, purchases, random items, location/data sharing, online interaction, unrestricted internet/UGC and user communication. Record maximum intensity, frequency, realism, context, player control and representative capture.

Include dormant but accessible assets, cheat/debug paths that ship, DLC and dynamically downloaded content. Marketing materials often have stricter general-audience rules than the product rating.

## Submission workflow

1. Choose target territories/storefronts and identify their current rating route.
2. Freeze a review candidate and complete the content inventory.
3. Capture the highest-intensity representative material and disclosures.
4. Have design, production and compliance owners review questionnaire answers.
5. Store certificates, answers, dates, build/content IDs and icon-use rules.
6. Re-evaluate every content or feature update against the questionnaire.

Google Play assigns locally relevant ratings from questionnaire answers and requires an update when content/features change those answers. Do not copy one territory's badge or descriptor into another context without verifying authorization and display rules.

## UGC and online features

Define moderation, reporting, blocking, filters, default privacy, age gates and escalation before enabling player content or communication. A filter does not erase the need to disclose the feature. Generative systems need bounded output, reporting and current platform-policy review.
