# Asset provenance, licensing and generative AI

Reviewed: 2026-08-29

This is production hygiene, not legal advice. Verify current licenses and obtain professional advice for material risk.

## Asset manifest

Track every external or generated asset with stable ID, file/hash, type, creator, source URL, acquisition date/account, exact license/version, receipt or evidence snapshot, attribution text, modification history, permitted engines/platforms, seat/entity restrictions, redistribution/source restrictions, AI/tool/model disclosure, training/reference inputs, reviewer and release status.

An asset marketplace purchase normally grants a license, not ownership. Do not copy marketplace source assets into a public knowledge/sample repository unless redistribution is explicitly permitted. Engine starter content and marketplace content may have different agreements.

## Generated content gate

Record tool/model/version, account/terms date, full prompt, seeds/settings when available, source/reference assets and rights, human edits, similarity review, identity/voice consent, provenance metadata and storefront disclosures. Avoid prompts requesting imitation of living artists or protected characters. Generated output still needs trademark, publicity/privacy, cultural and platform review.

## Release gate

No unknown-license files; attribution bundle generated; incompatible/copyleft obligations reviewed; notices shipped; source obligations fulfilled; storefront AI/content survey accurate; removal/replacement path exists.
