# Store page, trailer and marketing evidence

Reviewed: 2026-08-29

Marketing should accurately compress the playable experience. Build capture hooks and a repeatable asset pipeline while the vertical slice is developed, not in the final release week.

## Message hierarchy

Define one player fantasy, three observable differentiators and the intended audience. Every capsule, screenshot, trailer beat and description should reinforce them with real gameplay evidence. Do not promise modes, visuals or performance that the shipping build cannot deliver.

## Capture-ready build

Provide reproducible save states or debug bookmarks, HUD-safe and HUD-visible modes, camera/capture controls, clean localization, stable frame pacing and a record of build/scene/settings used. Preserve source captures and licenses for music, fonts, logos and art.

## Steam-oriented baseline

Current Steam guidance requires real gameplay screenshots and recommends placing a primarily gameplay trailer first. Viewers may decide within seconds or watch muted, so reveal the actual interaction early and make the premise understandable without narration. Steam generates its microtrailer from the first visible trailer.

Base capsules are limited to game artwork, name and official subtitle; marketing copy and awards belong in their dedicated store fields. Always download current platform templates instead of copying dimensions from an old checklist.

## Asset matrix

Track for each storefront: required size/format, safe area, localized text, maturity rules, source file, export preset, owner, due date and upload status. Include capsule/key art, icon, logo, screenshots, trailer/poster, description, feature list, supported languages, accessibility, system requirements, ratings and disclosures.

## Review gates

- A stranger can describe the genre, player action and differentiator after ten seconds.
- Screenshots come from a representative current build and show readable UI where useful.
- Trailer footage, claims and store metadata match the release candidate.
- Text and text-bearing art are localized consistently.
- All assets pass platform rules, provenance review and age-rating constraints.
- The final published page is checked on desktop/mobile surfaces and major locales.
