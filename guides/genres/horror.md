# Horror game playbook

Reviewed: 2026-08-29

## Product loop

Explore under uncertainty → notice threat evidence → choose avoidance/resource use → endure escalation → reach temporary safety → reinterpret the world. Fear depends on anticipation, consequence and trust in rules, not constant surprise.

## Smallest vertical slice

One atmospheric route with objective, one threat grammar, one resource constraint, safe/readable interaction, chase or confrontation, fail/restart/checkpoint, audio/lighting baseline and motion/flashing/accessibility settings.

## High-risk contracts

- threat perception/search/chase rules and fair loss conditions;
- lighting/audio that preserve required navigation and interaction cues;
- tension director with cooldown, intensity bounds and deterministic debug mode;
- checkpoint placement that avoids repetitive frustration;
- camera/FOV/head-bob/shake and motion-reduction alternatives;
- content warnings, photosensitivity and volume/dynamic-range options.

## Test route

Players who rush, hide indefinitely, backtrack, miss the key clue, mute audio, raise brightness, use subtitles/reduced motion and fail repeatedly. Verify recovery and objective clarity without developer coaching.

## Typical traps

Untelegraphed instant death, darkness as the only obstacle, jump-scare saturation, threat cheating visibly, key information only in audio/color, long repeated walks after death and settings that destroy either playability or intended hierarchy.
